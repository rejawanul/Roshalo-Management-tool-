Privacy Policy

Roshalo Business Manager — last updated 22 November 2026.

What the app is

Roshalo is a private desktop application used by the owners of Roshalo Premium Nut Mix to keep their own business records. All business data is stored on the owner's own computer.

What Google access it asks for

If the owner chooses to connect Google Drive, the app asks for the drive.file permission only. This lets the app see and manage only the files and folders that the app itself creates (a "Roshalo Reports" folder with report PDFs, and a "Roshalo Backups" folder with encrypted backups). It cannot see any other file in the owner's Google Drive.

What is stored and where

  • Business records stay in a database on the owner's computer.
  • Google sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • Report PDFs and encrypted backups are uploaded only to the owner's own Google Drive.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to anyone. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google Drive inside the app at any time, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
